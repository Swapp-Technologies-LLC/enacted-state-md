# Anne Arundel County DPW (Crofton/Odenton) — Lead and Copper Violation (2025 Report)

## 1. As Reported
- **Reporting Agency:** Anne Arundel County Department of Public Works
- **Water System:** Crofton/Odenton Water Service Area
- **Reporting Period:** October 1 through 14, 2025 (Published in 2025 Water Quality Report)
- **Publication Date:** June 1, 2026
- **Finding:** Monitoring and reporting violation assessed by the Maryland Department of the Environment
- **Governing Requirement:** Minimum 30 lead and copper samples collected and submitted by the reporting deadline
- **Compliance Period:** October 1 through 14, 2025

## 2. Verbatim Quoted Source Text
From Anne Arundel County Water Quality Report 2025 (page 4):
```
During fall 2025, we were required to collect and analyze a minimum of 30 lead and copper samples from the Crofton/Odenton water service area. Samples were sent to a contracted laboratory for analysis. Results for two samples were not received in time to meet the reporting deadline. As a result, we were assessed a monitoring and reporting violation for October 1 through 14, 2025.
```
