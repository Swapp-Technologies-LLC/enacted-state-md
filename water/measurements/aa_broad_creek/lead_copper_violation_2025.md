# Anne Arundel County DPW (Broad Creek) — Lead and Copper Violation (2025 Report)

## 1. As Reported
- **Reporting Agency:** Anne Arundel County Department of Public Works
- **Water System:** Broad Creek Water Service Area
- **Reporting Period:** October 1 through 14, 2025 (Published in 2025 Water Quality Report)
- **Finding:** Monitoring and reporting violation assessed by the Maryland Department of the Environment
- **Governing Requirement:** Minimum 30 lead and copper samples collected and submitted by the reporting deadline
- **Compliance Period:** October 1 through 14, 2025

## 2. Verbatim Quoted Source Text
From Anne Arundel County Water Quality Report 2025 (page 4):
```
During fall 2025, we were required to collect and analyze a minimum of 30 lead and copper samples from the Broad Creek water service area. We collected 36 samples, but results were not received from the contracted laboratory for 8 of the 36 samples. We immediately collected additional samples, but the results of the analyses were not submitted until several days after the reporting deadline. As a result, we were assessed a monitoring and reporting violation for October 1 through 14, 2025.
```
