# Carroll County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Carroll County Board of County Commissioners
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Carroll County Code Chapter 158 (Zoning Regulations)**
- **Planning Agency:** Carroll County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** A (Agricultural), C (Conservation), R-40,000, R-20,000, R-10,000, B-G (Business General), I-R (Industrial Restricted)
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.carrollcountymd.gov/government/directory/land-resource-management/planning/).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.carrollcountymd.gov/government/directory/technology-services/gis-mapping/.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Carroll County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`westminster/`**: City of Westminster — Autonomous Zoning Code Chapter 164
- **`mount_airy/`**: Town of Mount Airy — Autonomous Municipal Zoning
- **`taneytown/`**: City of Taneytown — Independent Planning & Zoning
