# Wicomico County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Wicomico County Council
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Wicomico County Code Chapter 225 (Zoning)**
- **Planning Agency:** Wicomico County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** A-1 (Agricultural-Rural), AR, R-20, R-15, LB-1 (Local Business), GB (General Business), I-1, I-2, Critical Area Overlay
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.wicomicocounty.org/137/Planning-Zoning).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.wicomicocounty.org/142/GIS.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Wicomico County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`salisbury/`**: City of Salisbury — Autonomous City Zoning Code Title 17
- **`fruitland/`**: City of Fruitland — Autonomous Municipal Zoning
