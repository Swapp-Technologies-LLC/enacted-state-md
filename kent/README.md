# Kent County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Kent County Board of County Commissioners
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Kent County Land Use Ordinance**
- **Planning Agency:** Kent County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** AZD (Agricultural Zoning District), R (Rural), RC (Resource Conservation), CC (Commercial Critical Area), V (Village), C (Commercial), M (Industrial)
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.kentcounty.com/planning).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.kentcounty.com/gis.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Kent County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`chestertown/`**: Town of Chestertown — Autonomous Municipal Land Use Code
- **`rock_hall/`**: Town of Rock Hall — Independent Waterfront Planning Commission
