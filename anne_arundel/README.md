# Anne Arundel County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority
- **Governing Body:** Anne Arundel County Council.
- **Primary Ordinance:** Anne Arundel County Code **Article 18 (Zoning)**.
- **Planning Agency:** Anne Arundel County Office of Planning and Zoning (OPZ).

## 2. Statutory Architecture (Article 18 Breakdown)
- **Title 1:** Definitions
- **Title 2:** General Provisions (§ 18-2-103 Comprehensive Zoning and Digital Zoning Layer)
- **Title 3:** Parking, Outdoor Lighting, and Sign Regulations
- **Title 4:** Residential Districts (RA, RLD, R1, R2, R5, R10, R15, R22)
- **Title 5:** Commercial Districts (C1, C2, C3, C4 use charts § 18-5-102)
- **Title 6:** Industrial Districts (W1, W2, W3)
- **Title 7:** Maritime Districts (MA1, MA2, MA3, MB)
- **Title 8:** Mixed Use Districts (MXD-C, MXD-E, MXD-R, MXD-T)
- **Title 9:** Other Zoning Districts (Open Space, Town Center, Small Area Plans)
- **Title 10:** Requirements for Conditional Uses
- **Title 11:** Requirements for Special Exception Uses
- **Title 12:** Special Uses
- **Title 13:** Critical Area Overlay (Chesapeake Bay Critical Area 1,000-foot buffer requirements)
- **Title 14:** Other Overlays (Bog Protection, Floodplain)
- **Title 15:** Nonconforming Uses
- **Title 16:** Administrative Hearings
- **Title 17:** Enforcement and Penalties
- **Title 18:** Fees

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Source:** Anne Arundel County Council Legislative Portal (`aacounty.org/county-council/legislation`) and Granicus/Legistar feed.
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Council Bills (e.g., Bill No. XX-YY) amending Article 18.
- **GIS & Parcel Boundaries:** Anne Arundel County OPZ Open Data Portal (`gis-aacounty.opendata.arcgis.com`).

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions
- **`annapolis/`**: The City of Annapolis is an independent incorporated city with autonomous zoning authority under **Title 21 (Planning and Zoning)** of the Annapolis City Code.
- **`highland_beach/`**: Incorporated municipal jurisdiction with independent town charter zoning.
- **Unincorporated Communities:** Glen Burnie, Severna Park, Crofton, Odenton, Pasadena, and Arnold are governed directly by County Article 18.
