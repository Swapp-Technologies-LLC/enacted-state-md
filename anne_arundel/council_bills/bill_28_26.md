# Anne Arundel County Bill No. 28-26

## 1. Official Legislative Summary
FOR the purpose of allowing "dwelling unit, apartment, as an accessory use provided the entrance is separate from the commercial use" as a permitted use in C4 zoning districts; and generally relating to zoning.

## 2. Legislative Chronology
- **Introduced:** April 6, 2026 (Mr. Volke)
- **Public Hearing:** May 4, 2026
- **Passed:** May 4, 2026 (unanimous, 7-0)
- **Presented to County Executive:** May 5, 2026
- **Approved and Enacted:** May 7, 2026 (Steuart Pittman, County Executive)
- **Effective Date:** June 21, 2026 (Section 2: 45 days from date it becomes law)

## 3. Statutory Text (Mechanically Quoted from Enacted Bill PDF)

```
ARTICLE 18. ZONING

TITLE 5. COMMERCIAL DISTRICTS

18-5-102. Permitted, conditional, special exception, and business complex auxiliary uses.

The permitted, conditional, and special exception uses allowed in each of the commercial
districts, and uses auxiliary to a business complex, are listed in the chart in this section
using the following key: P = permitted use; C = conditional use; SE = special exception use;
and A = auxiliary to a business complex use. A blank means that the use is not allowed in
the district. Except as provided otherwise in this article, uses and structures customarily
accessory to permitted, conditional, and special exception uses also are allowed.

Permitted, conditional, special exception, and           C1  C2  C3  C4
business complex auxiliary uses
***
Dwelling unit, apartment, as an accessory use            P   P   P   P
provided the entrance is separate from the
commercial use
***
```

## 4. Statutory Divergence Note
The title and purpose clause of Bill No. 28-26 state: *"FOR the purpose of allowing 'dwelling unit, apartment, as an accessory use provided the entrance is separate from the commercial use' as a permitted use in C4 zoning districts."* However, the enacted use chart in Section 1 reenacts the entire row showing **P** under all four commercial columns (**C1, C2, C3, and C4**). Where the title summary and the enacted statutory chart diverge, the enacted statutory text controls.
