# Anne Arundel County Code — Article 18: Title 12. Special Uses

**Jurisdiction:** Anne Arundel County, Maryland  
**Governing Body:** Anne Arundel County Council  
**Administrative Body:** Office of Planning and Zoning (OPZ)  
**Primary Source:** Anne Arundel County Code (2005, as amended)

---

## Overview
Temporary uses, adult entertainment, housing for the elderly, and telecommunications towers.
