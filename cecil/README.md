# Cecil County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Cecil County Council
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Cecil County Land Use Code (Zoning Ordinance)**
- **Planning Agency:** Cecil County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** NAR (Northern Agricultural Residential), SAR (Southern Agricultural Residential), RR (Rural Residential), LDR, RM, BG, BI, M1, M2, Critical Area Overlay
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.ccgov.org/government/land-use-development-services).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.ccgov.org/government/information-technology/gis-maps.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Cecil County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`elkton/`**: Town of Elkton — Autonomous Municipal Zoning Chapter 17
- **`perryville/`**: Town of Perryville — Independent Planning Commission
- **`north_east/`**: Town of North East — Autonomous Town Zoning
