# Charles County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Charles County Board of County Commissioners
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Charles County Code Chapter 297 (Zoning)**
- **Planning Agency:** Charles County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** AC (Agricultural Conservation), RC (Rural Conservation), RL (Rural Low), WCD (Watershed Conservation District), CC (Commercial Core), IG, IH, Critical Area
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.charlescountymd.gov/government/planning-and-growth-management).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.charlescountymd.gov/government/information-technology/geographic-information-system-gis.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Charles County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`la_plata/`**: Town of La Plata — Autonomous Municipal Zoning Code Chapter 191
- **`indian_head/`**: Town of Indian Head — Independent Planning Commission
