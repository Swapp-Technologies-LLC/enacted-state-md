# St. Mary's County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** St. Mary's County Board of County Commissioners
- **Primary Ordinance:** **St. Mary's County Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance (CZO Chapter 285)**
- **Planning Agency:** St. Mary's County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** RPD (Rural Preservation District - 1 unit/5 acres), RL, RH, CMX, DMX, I (Industrial), AICUZ (Naval Air Station Patuxent River Air Installation Compatible Use Zone Overlay), Critical Area
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.stmaryscountymd.gov/lugm/).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.stmaryscountymd.gov/it/gis/.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in St. Mary's County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`leonardtown/`**: Town of Leonardtown — Autonomous Municipal Zoning Code Chapter 155
