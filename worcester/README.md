# Worcester County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Worcester County Board of County Commissioners
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Worcester County Code Title 1 (Zoning)**
- **Planning Agency:** Worcester County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** A-1, A-2 (Agricultural), E-1 (Estate), R-1, R-2, R-3 (Residential), C-1, C-2 (Commercial), I-1, Atlantic Coastal Bays Critical Area Overlay
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.co.worcester.md.us/departments/development).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.co.worcester.md.us/departments/it/gis.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Worcester County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`ocean_city/`**: Town of Ocean City — Autonomous Municipal Code Chapter 110 (Zoning)
- **`berlin/`**: Town of Berlin — Autonomous Municipal Planning & Historic Zoning
- **`snow_hill/`**: Town of Snow Hill — Independent Town Planning Commission
