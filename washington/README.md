# Washington County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Washington County Board of County Commissioners
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Washington County Zoning Ordinance**
- **Planning Agency:** Washington County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** A(R) (Agricultural Rural), EC (Environmental Conservation), RS, RU, RT, BG (Business General), BL, HI (Highway Interchange), IR, IG
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.washco-md.net/planning-zoning/).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.washco-md.net/gis-mapping/.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Washington County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`hagerstown/`**: City of Hagerstown — Autonomous Land Management Code (LMC) Chapter 140
- **`boonsboro/`**: Town of Boonsboro — Autonomous Municipal Planning
- **`williamsport/`**: Town of Williamsport — Independent Town Planning
