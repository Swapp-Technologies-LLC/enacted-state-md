# Dorchester County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Dorchester County Council
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Dorchester County Code Chapter 155 (Zoning)**
- **Planning Agency:** Dorchester County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** A (Agricultural), AC (Agricultural Conservation), B-1, B-2, I-1, I-2, Extensive Chesapeake Bay Critical Area (IDA, LDA, RCA)
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.dorchestercountymd.com/departments/planning-zoning/).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.dorchestercountymd.com/departments/information-technology/gis/.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Dorchester County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`cambridge/`**: City of Cambridge — Autonomous Unified Development Code (UDC)
- **`hurlock/`**: Town of Hurlock — Independent Planning Commission
