# Calvert County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Calvert County Board of County Commissioners
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Calvert County Zoning Ordinance (CCZO)**
- **Planning Agency:** Calvert County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** FFD (Farm and Forest District), RD (Residential District), TC (Town Center), I-1 (Light Industrial), Critical Area Overlay
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.calvertcountymd.gov/117/Planning-Zoning).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.calvertcountymd.gov/146/Geographic-Information-Systems-GIS.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Calvert County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`chesapeake_beach/`**: Town of Chesapeake Beach — Autonomous Municipal Zoning
- **`north_beach/`**: Town of North Beach — Independent Town Zoning
