# Baltimore County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Baltimore County Council (7 council districts).
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Baltimore County Zoning Regulations (BCZR)**.
- **Planning & Permitting Agencies:**
  - Baltimore County Department of Planning (`baltimorecountymd.gov/departments/planning`)
  - Department of Permits, Approvals and Inspections (PAI)
- **Unique Quadrennial Review:** **Comprehensive Zoning Map Process (CZMP)** — held every 4 years (e.g., 2020, 2024, 2028), allowing property owners and citizens to request zoning reclassifications across the entire county in a single unified legislative cycle.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture (BCZR Breakdown)
The BCZR establishes distinct density, dimensional, and environmental classifications:
- **Article 1: Residential Zones**:
  - *Density Zones:* D.R.1 (1 unit/acre), D.R.2, D.R.3.5, D.R.5.5 (standard suburban), D.R.10.5, D.R.16 (multifamily/townhouse)
  - *Rural & Resource Conservation:* R.C.2 (Agricultural preservation - 1 unit/50 acres), R.C.3 (River basin), R.C.4 (Watershed protection), R.C.5 (Rural residential), R.C.6, R.C.7, R.C.8
- **Article 2: Commercial Zones**:
  - B.L. (Business Local), B.M. (Business Major), B.R. (Business Roadside), B.A. (Business Automotive)
- **Article 3: Industrial & Manufacturing Zones**:
  - M.L. (Manufacturing Light), M.H. (Manufacturing Heavy), M.L.R. (Manufacturing Light Restricted)
- **Article 4: Special Purpose & Mixed-Use**:
  - C.T. (Commercial, Town Center - e.g. Towson, Owings Mills), C.C.C. (Commercial, Community Center), O.T. (Office, Town)
- **Article 5: Parking, Signs, and General Regulations**
- **Critical Area Overlay:** Chesapeake Bay Critical Area IDA, LDA, and RCA buffer management along the 200+ miles of tidal shoreline.

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Source:** Baltimore County Council Legislative System (`baltimorecountymd.gov/countycouncil`).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Council Bills (e.g., Bill No. XX-YY).
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** Baltimore County GIS Open Data (`gis-baltimorecountymd.opendata.arcgis.com`) and MyNeighborhood mapping portal.

---

## 4. Municipal Structure: 100% Unincorporated County
Unlike most U.S. counties, **Baltimore County contains ZERO incorporated municipalities**. All 850,000+ residents live in unincorporated communities governed directly by the Baltimore County Council and BCZR:
- **Major Urban Centers:** Towson (County Seat), Catonsville, Dundalk, Essex, Owings Mills, White Marsh, Middle River, Pikesville, Parkville, Randallstown, Cockeysville, Hunt Valley, Perry Hall, Woodlawn, Arbutus.
