# Baltimore County Bill No. 9-26
**Status:** Enacted by the County Council of Baltimore County  
**Enacted Date:** February 23, 2026  
**Effective Date:** April 6, 2026  

## Summary of Enacted Changes
Bill No. 9-26 amends the Baltimore County Zoning Regulations (BCZR) concerning data centers and energy storage systems, partially repealing provisions from Bill 54-24 and establishing stricter locational setbacks and environmental screening standards in manufacturing and industrial zones.
