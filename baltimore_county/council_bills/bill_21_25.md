# Baltimore County Bill No. 21-25
**Status:** Enacted by the County Council of Baltimore County  
**Enacted Date:** April 14, 2025  
**Effective Date:** May 26, 2025  

## Summary of Enacted Changes
Bill No. 21-25 amends BCZR Section 4F regarding commercial ground-mounted solar energy systems in rural and agricultural zones, establishing enhanced buffer yards and vegetative screening requirements adjacent to residential parcels.

```text
A BILL ENTITLED
AN ACT concerning
Zoning Regulations - Solar Facilities - Siting Requirements
FOR the purpose of amending the zoning regulations for commercial solar energy systems;
modifying setback and buffer requirements; requiring vegetative screening; and generally
relating to solar energy systems.

SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF BALTIMORE COUNTY, MARYLAND, that
Section 4F102 of the Baltimore County Zoning Regulations is amended to require a minimum
setback of 100 feet from any adjacent residential property line.
```
