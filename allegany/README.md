# Allegany County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Allegany County Board of County Commissioners
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Allegany County Land Development Code**
- **Planning Agency:** Allegany County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** A (Agricultural), R (Residential), C (Commercial), I (Industrial), P (Park/Conservation)
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.alleganygov.org/307/Land-Development).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.alleganygov.org/311/GIS-Mapping.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Allegany County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`cumberland/`**: City of Cumberland — Autonomous Zoning Code Chapter 25
- **`frostburg/`**: City of Frostburg — Independent Planning & Zoning
