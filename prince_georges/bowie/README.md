# City of Bowie — Planning & Advisory Design Review Jurisdiction

## 1. Regulatory Authority
- **Governing Body:** Bowie City Council / Bowie Advisory Planning Board
- **Applicable Code:** Prince George's County Subtitle 27 (Zoning) + City of Bowie Development Review Guidelines
- **Autonomous Zoning Power:** NO (Governed by County Subtitle 27 with municipal advisory/delegated review)

## 2. Jurisdictional Boundary & Statutory Notes
Operates under County Subtitle 27. Under a memorandum of understanding with M-NCPPC, the City of Bowie exercises municipal concurrent review on preliminary subdivision plans, variances, and architectural design standards within incorporated city limits.

## 3. Primary Ingestion Sources
- **Official City Portal:** Primary municipal records and council agenda minutes.
- **GIS Parcels:** Coordinated via PGAtlas (`pgatlas.com`) and municipal GIS maps.
