# City of Laurel — Autonomous Municipal Zoning Jurisdiction

## 1. Regulatory Authority
- **Governing Body:** City of Laurel Mayor and City Council / Laurel Planning Commission
- **Applicable Code:** City of Laurel Unified Land Development Code (ULDC) Chapter 20
- **Autonomous Zoning Power:** YES (Completely exempt from County Subtitle 27)

## 2. Jurisdictional Boundary & Statutory Notes
Pursuant to Md. Code Ann., Land Use § 20-701, the City of Laurel is entirely EXEMPT from M-NCPPC Regional District zoning authority and Prince George's County Subtitle 27. Laurel maintains its own zoning districts (R-55, R-T, C-V, I-CS), board of appeals, and comprehensive master plan.
