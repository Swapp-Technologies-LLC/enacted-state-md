# City of College Park — Revitalization & Parking Authority Jurisdiction

## 1. Regulatory Authority
- **Governing Body:** College Park Mayor and City Council
- **Applicable Code:** Prince George's County Subtitle 27 + City of College Park Code Chapter 110 & 87
- **Autonomous Zoning Power:** NO (Governed by County Subtitle 27 with municipal advisory/delegated review)

## 2. Jurisdictional Boundary & Statutory Notes
Operates under County Subtitle 27 with active participation in the Route 1 / UMD Discovery District Revitalization Overlay and municipal student-housing/parking regulations.

## 3. Primary Ingestion Sources
- **Official City Portal:** Primary municipal records and council agenda minutes.
- **GIS Parcels:** Coordinated via PGAtlas (`pgatlas.com`) and municipal GIS maps.
