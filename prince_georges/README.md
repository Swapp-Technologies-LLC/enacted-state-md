# Prince George's County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Prince George's County Council (sitting as the District Council for that portion of the Maryland-Washington Regional District located within Prince George's County under Md. Code Ann., Land Use § 22-101 et seq.).
- **Primary Ordinance:** Prince George's County Code **Subtitle 27 (Zoning Ordinance)**.
- **Comprehensive Rewrite:** Fully overhauled and replaced the legacy 50-year-old code effective **April 1, 2022** via County Council Bill **CB-013-2019** / **CB-090-2020** (modernized zones, consolidated use tables, and transit-oriented development focus).
- **Planning Agency:** Prince George's County Planning Department (M-NCPPC — Maryland-National Capital Park and Planning Commission).

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture (Subtitle 27 Breakdown)
Subtitle 27 is organized into 9 primary parts:
- **Part 27-1: General Provisions & Interpretation** (Definitions, nonconformities, transitional provisions from legacy zones)
- **Part 27-2: Administration & Procedures** (Zoning Text Amendments, Zoning Map Amendments, Special Exceptions, Variances, Minor Amendments)
- **Part 27-3: Zones & Zone Regulations**:
  - *Rural & Agricultural Zones:* AR (Agricultural/Residential), RE (Rural Estate)
  - *Residential Zones:* RR (Rural Residential), R-E (Residential Estate), R-R (Rural Residential Single-Family), R-80, R-55 (Single-Family Detached), R-35 (Small Lot), R-20, R-T (Townhouse), R-18, R-10 (Multifamily)
  - *Commercial Zones:* CN (Commercial Neighborhood), CS (Commercial Service), CGO (Commercial General Office), CG (Commercial General)
  - *Industrial Zones:* IE (Industrial Employment), IL (Industrial Light), IH (Industrial Heavy)
  - *Mixed-Use & Transit-Oriented Zones:* R-P-C (Planned Community), NAC (Neighborhood Activity Center), TAC (Town Activity Center), MAC (Major Activity Center), RTO (Regional Transit-Oriented)
- **Part 27-4: Use Regulations & Principal Use Table** (Permitted by right, special exception, temporary uses)
- **Part 27-5: Development Standards** (Dimensional standards, building heights, setbacks, lot coverage, floor area ratio)
- **Part 27-6: Site Development Standards** (Parking and loading, landscaping, tree canopy coverage, lighting, signage)
- **Part 27-7: Subdivision Regulations (Subtitle 24 Coordination)**
- **Part 27-8: Overlay & Planned Development Zones** (Aviation Policy Area APA, Military Installation Overlay MIO, Neighborhood Conservation Overlay NCO, Transit District Overlay TDO)
- **Part 27-9: Violations, Penalties & Enforcement**

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Planning Jurisdictions
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division II, Prince George's County contains **27 incorporated municipalities**. Their jurisdictional zoning powers are strictly partitioned by state law:

### A. Autonomous Municipal Zoning Authority (Exempt from Subtitle 27)
- **`laurel/` (City of Laurel):** Sole municipality in Prince George's County with independent planning and zoning jurisdiction. Governed by the **City of Laurel Unified Land Development Code (ULDC)**. M-NCPPC and County Subtitle 27 have zero zoning authority within Laurel city limits.

### B. Municipalities Under County Subtitle 27 with Concurrent/Delegated Authority
The remaining 26 municipalities operate within the Maryland-Washington Regional District under Subtitle 27, with varying levels of municipal review authority:
- **`bowie/` (City of Bowie):** Largest municipality; exercises delegated preliminary subdivision review and design review authority.
- **`college_park/` (City of College Park):** University of Maryland campus anchor; exercises municipal parking authority and Revitalization Overlay review.
- **`greenbelt/` (City of Greenbelt):** Historic cooperative garden city; concurrent historic district and advisory site plan review.
- **`hyattsville/` (City of Hyattsville):** Gateway Arts District anchor; urban development and commercial revitalization overlay review.
- **`cheverly/`, `mount_rainier/`, `riverdale_park/`, `upper_marlboro/`**: Municipal advisory planning bodies with localized historic/commercial overlay reviews.
