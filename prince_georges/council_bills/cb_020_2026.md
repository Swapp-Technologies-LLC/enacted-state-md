# Prince George's County CB-020-2026
**Status:** Enacted by the County Council sitting as District Council  
**Passed & Enacted:** June 22, 2026  
**Effective Date:** December 21, 2026  

## Summary of Enacted Changes
CB-020-2026 amends Subtitle 27 (Zoning Ordinance) to establish mandatory pre-application neighborhood meeting requirements and public notification protocols for applicants seeking major land use development approvals in Prince George's County.
