# Prince George's County CB-015-2024
**Status:** Enacted by the County Council sitting as District Council  
**Passed & Enacted:** July 16, 2024  
**Effective Date:** September 3, 2024  

## Summary of Enacted Changes
CB-015-2024 is the comprehensive 2024 Zoning Ordinance Omnibus Bill for Prince George's County, reconciling statutory definitions, administrative procedures, and use regulations across Chapter 27 (the County's modernized zoning ordinance).
