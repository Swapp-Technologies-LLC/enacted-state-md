# Baltimore City, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Baltimore City Council.
- **Constitutional Status:** Independent City under Article XI-A of the Maryland Constitution (exercises concurrent county-level and municipal-level planning, zoning, and police powers; not part of any county).
- **Primary Ordinance:** Baltimore City Code **Article 32 (Zoning)**, enacted under the comprehensive "Transform Baltimore" overhaul effective **June 5, 2017** (City Council Bill 12-0152 / Ordinance 16-581).
- **Planning Agency:** Baltimore City Department of Planning & Planning Commission.
- **Board of Appeals:** Baltimore City Board of Municipal and Zoning Appeals (BMZA).

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture (Article 32 Breakdown)
Article 32 contains 19 core titles:
- **Title 1:** Definitions; Rules of Construction
- **Title 2:** Nonconformities (Uses, structures, lots, signs)
- **Title 3:** Administration (Department of Planning, Planning Commission, BMZA)
- **Title 5:** Applications and Authorizations (Conditional Uses, Variances)
- **Title 7:** General Development Standards
- **Title 8:** Open Space and Environmental Performance Standards (Critical Area, Forest Conservation)
- **Title 9:** Residential Districts (R-1 through R-10, rowhouse row dimensional standards)
- **Title 10:** Commercial Districts (C-1 Neighborhood Business, C-1-E Community Business, C-2 Community Commercial, C-3 Commuter Commercial, C-4 Downtown Commercial, C-5 Downtown Arts)
- **Title 11:** Industrial Districts (I-MU Industrial Mixed-Use, I-1 Light Industrial, I-2 General Industrial)
- **Title 12:** Special Purpose Districts (EC Educational Campus, H Hospital, W Waterfront, TOD Transit-Oriented Development)
- **Title 13:** Overlay Districts (Design Review Overlay, Landmark Overlay, Port Overlay)
- **Title 14:** Use Standards (Permitted, conditional, accessory, temporary uses)
- **Title 15:** Site Plan Review
- **Title 16:** Off-Street Parking and Loading
- **Title 17:** Signs
- **Title 18:** Enforcement and Penalties
- **Title 19:** Fees

## 4. Sub-Districts & Special Planning Overlay Zones
Because Baltimore City is an independent city with no incorporated municipal subdivisions within its boundaries, its zoning taxonomy is divided into key overlay and master plan development sectors:
- **`waterfront_overlay/`**: Inner Harbor, Harbor East, Fells Point, Canton, and Port Covington / Peninsula Baltimore maritime setbacks.
- **`tod_districts/`**: Transit-Oriented Development overlays around Metro Subway, Light RailLink, and Penn Station / West Baltimore MARC stations.
- **`critical_area/`**: Critical Area 1,000-ft buffer management program along the Patapsco River and Baltimore Harbor.
- **`historic_districts/`**: Commission for Historical and Architectural Preservation (CHAP) exterior design review districts.
