# Baltimore City Ordinance 26-128 (Council Bill 26-0158)
**Status:** Enacted by the Mayor and City Council of Baltimore  
**Approved by Mayor:** June 16, 2026  
**Effective Date:** June 16, 2026  

## Summary of Enacted Changes
Ordinance 26-128 amends Baltimore City Code Article 32 (Zoning) to establish a moratorium on data centers by designating data centers as a prohibited use throughout the City pending health equity assessments and comprehensive land use reviews.

SECTION 1. BE IT ORDAINED BY THE MAYOR AND CITY COUNCIL OF BALTIMORE, That the
Baltimore City Code (Edition 2000), as amended, is hereby amended by adding new
definitions to Title 1, Subtitle 3, and amending Title 14, Subtitle 3 of Article 32 -
Zoning, to read as follows:

Article 32. Zoning
Title 1. Definitions; General Provisions
Subtitle 3. Definitions

§ 1-304. Use types.
(s-1) Data center.
"Data center" means a facility used primarily for housing computer systems and
associated components, such as telecommunications and storage systems, for the purpose
of processing, storing, and managing large amounts of electronic data.

Title 14. Use Standards
Subtitle 3. Commercial, Industrial, and Other Uses

§ 14-311. Data centers.
(a) In general.
Except as provided in subsection (b) of this section, a data center is a prohibited use in
all zoning districts.
