# Baltimore City Ordinance 26-146 (Council Bill 26-0177)
**Status:** Enacted by the Mayor and City Council of Baltimore  
**Approved by Mayor:** August 10, 2026  
**Effective Date:** August 10, 2026  

## Summary of Enacted Changes
Ordinance 26-146 amends the Zoning District Maps of Article 32 (Zoning) to change the zoning classification for the property known as 901 S Caton Avenue (Block 2108C, Lot 001) from the I-1 Light Industrial Zoning District to the C-2 Community Commercial Zoning District.

## Verbatim Enacted Provisions
Mechanically extracted from primary PDF (`source_sha256: 27862dae61ae6e65cfa29836338d62ac8c14a5dab88b06f716fea104d36f79da`):

```text
SECTION 1. BE IT ORDAINED BY THE MAYOR AND CITY COUNCIL OF BALTIMORE, That the
Zoning District Maps of the City of Baltimore, as referred to in Article 32 - Zoning,
§ 2-101 of the Baltimore City Code, are amended by changing from the I-1 Light
Industrial Zoning District to the C-2 Community Commercial Zoning District the property
known as 901 S Caton Avenue, as outlined in red on the plat accompanying this Ordinance.

SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ORDAINED, That this Ordinance takes effect on the 30th
day after the date it is enacted.
```
