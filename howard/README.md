# Howard County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Howard County Council (sitting as the Zoning Board of Howard County).
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Howard County Zoning Regulations (HCZR)** and Howard County Code **Title 16 (Planning, Zoning, Subdivisions and Land Development)**.
- **Planning Agency:** Howard County Department of Planning and Zoning (DPZ).
- **Appeals Body:** Howard County Hearing Examiner / Board of Appeals.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture (HCZR Breakdown)
The HCZR is structured around planned communities, rural preservation, and urban employment centers:
- **Residential Zones:**
  - *Rural Conservation:* RC (Rural Conservation - 1 unit/4.25 acres), RR (Rural Residential)
  - *Low Density:* R-20 (Single-family detached), R-12, R-ED (Estate District)
  - *Medium/High Density:* R-SA-8 (Single-family attached), R-A-15 (Apartments), R-VH (Village High)
- **The Columbia Planned Community (NT Zone - New Town):**
  - Unique zoning district covering the master-planned city of Columbia (~100,000 residents). Land use is governed by the Comprehensive Final Development Plan (FDP) phases and Columbia Association covenants.
- **Commercial & Mixed-Use Zones:**
  - B-1 (Business Local), B-2 (Business General), SC (Shopping Center), BR (Business Rural)
  - TOD (Transit Oriented Development - Laurel Race Track, Savage MARC)
  - Downtown Columbia (DT) zoning district with specific sustainability and affordable housing tiers.
- **Industrial & Employment:**
  - M-1 (Light Industrial), M-2 (Heavy Industrial), PEC (Planned Employment Center)

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Source:** Howard County Council Legislative Information Portal (`cc.howardcountymd.gov`).
- **Enacted Instruments:**
  - **Council Bills (CB):** County legislative acts amending Title 16.
  - **Zoning Board Petitions (ZRA):** Zoning Regulation Amendments enacting text changes to HCZR.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** Howard County Open Data / DPZ Interactive Mapping (`data.howardcountymd.gov` and `maps.howardcountymd.gov`).

---

## 4. Municipal Structure: Zero Incorporated Municipalities
Like Baltimore County, **Howard County has zero incorporated cities or towns**.
- **Columbia:** Unincorporated planned community administered via DPZ NT zoning and Columbia Association village boards.
- **Ellicott City:** Unincorporated historic county seat; subject to County Historic Preservation Commission (HPC) and Historic District Commission guidelines.
- **Other Communities:** Elkridge, Marriottsville, Clarksville, Fulton/Maple Lawn, Dayton, Glenelg, Woodbine.
