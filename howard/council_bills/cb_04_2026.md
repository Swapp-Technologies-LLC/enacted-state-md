# Howard County Council Bill 4-2026 (ZRA 219)

## 1. Official Legislative Summary
AN ACT amending the Howard County Zoning Regulations to implement the General Plan's recommendations for housing in activity centers, including defining activity center; providing that certain types of development projects in the TOD or TNC zoning districts shall provide more than one housing type; and adding regulations for age-restricted adult housing in the TOD and TNC zoning districts; and generally relating to the Howard County Zoning Regulations.

## 2. Legislative Chronology
- **Introduced:** January 5, 2026 (Legislative Day No. 1, by The Chairperson at the request of the County Executive)
- **Public Hearing:** January 20, 2026
- **Tabled:** February 2, 2026
- **Removed from Table & Withdrawn:** March 2, 2026
- **Outcome:** Did not become law. Existing TOD and TNC district regulations remain in effect.

## 3. Statutory Text Context (Proposal Withdrawn)
The proposed text sought to define "activity center" and mandate diverse housing types for development projects 5 gross acres or greater in TOD and TNC districts. Because the bill was withdrawn on March 2, 2026, no amendments took effect.
