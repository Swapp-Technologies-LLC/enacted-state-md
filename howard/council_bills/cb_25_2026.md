# Howard County Council Bill 25-2026 (ZRA 224)

## 1. Official Legislative Summary
AN ACT amending the Howard County Zoning Regulations to define "Cottage Clusters"; to provide where Age-Restricted Adult Housing Cottage Clusters will be permitted by right; to add parking requirements to Age-Restricted Adult Housing Cottage Clusters and multiplexes; to provide criteria for Age-Restricted Adult Housing Cottage Clusters within an Optional Design Project; and generally relating to the Howard County Zoning Regulations.

## 2. Legislative Chronology
- **Introduced:** May 4, 2026 (Legislative Day No. 6, by The Chairperson at the request of the County Executive)
- **Public Hearing:** May 18, 2026
- **Passed as Amended:** June 1, 2026
- **Executive Action (Signed):** June 10, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 10, 2026

## 3. Statutory Text (Verbatim Quoted from Enacted Bill PDF)

```
Howard County Zoning Regulations
Section 103.0. Definitions.

COTTAGE CLUSTER: A GROUPING, INCLUDING A COMMON COURTYARD, OF AT LEAST FOUR SINGLE
STORY DETACHED DWELLING UNITS THAT HAVE A FOOTPRINT OF NO MORE THAN 1,200 SQUARE
FEET AND DOES NOT INCLUDE A BASEMENT.
```
