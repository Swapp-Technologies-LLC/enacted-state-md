# Somerset County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Somerset County Board of County Commissioners
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Somerset County Code Chapter 9 (Zoning)**
- **Planning Agency:** Somerset County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** AR (Agricultural Residential), MRC (Maritime Residential Commercial), I-1, I-2, Extensive Coastal Critical Area Overlays
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.somersetmd.us/departments/planning_and_zoning/index.php).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.somersetmd.us/departments/gis_mapping/index.php.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Somerset County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`crisfield/`**: City of Crisfield — Autonomous Waterfront Zoning
- **`princess_anne/`**: Town of Princess Anne — Independent Town Planning Commission
