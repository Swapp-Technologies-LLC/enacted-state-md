# Garrett County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Garrett County Board of County Commissioners
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Garrett County Deep Creek Watershed Zoning Ordinance & County Planning Standards**
- **Planning Agency:** Garrett County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** LR (Lake Residential), CR (Commercial Resort), TR (Town Residential), AR (Agricultural Resource), Watershed Protection Overlays
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.garrettcountymd.gov/planning-land-management).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.garrettcountymd.gov/gis.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Garrett County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`oakland/`**: Town of Oakland — Autonomous Municipal Zoning
- **`grantsville/`**: Town of Grantsville — Independent Planning Review
