# Queen Anne's County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Queen Anne's County Board of County Commissioners
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Queen Anne's County Code Chapter 18:1 (Zoning & Resource Protection)**
- **Planning Agency:** Queen Anne's County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** AG (Agricultural), CS (Countryside), NC (Neighborhood Conservation), UC (Urban Commercial), SI (Suburban Industrial), Critical Area Overlay
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.qac.org/214/Planning-Zoning).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.qac.org/272/GIS-Mapping.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Queen Anne's County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`centreville/`**: Town of Centreville — Autonomous Municipal Zoning Chapter 170
- **`queenstown/`**: Town of Queenstown — Independent Municipal Planning Commission
