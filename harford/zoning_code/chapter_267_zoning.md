## Overview of Governed Regulations
Chapter 267 establishes comprehensive zoning controls for Harford County, governing:
* Agricultural, Residential, Commercial, and Industrial Zoning District regulations
* Permitted and Special Exception Uses
* Development standards, setbacks, buffer yards, and building height restrictions
* Natural Resource District (NRD) and Chesapeake Bay Critical Area overlay standards

HARFORD COUNTY DEVELOPMENT REGULATIONS
CHAPTER 267. ZONING
ARTICLE I. GENERAL PROVISIONS
§ 267-1. Title.
This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the "Harford County Zoning Code."

§ 267-2. Purpose and intent.
The purpose of this chapter is to promote the health, safety, morals, and general welfare
of the present and future inhabitants of Harford County by dividing the county into
districts and regulating the location, height, bulk, and size of buildings and other
structures, the size of yards, courts, and other open spaces, and the density of population.
