# Harford County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Harford County Council
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Harford County Code Chapter 267 (Zoning)**
- **Planning Agency:** Harford County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** AG (Agricultural), RR (Rural Residential), R1, R2, R3, R4 (Urban Residential), B1, B2, B3 (Business), CI (Commercial Industrial), MO (Mixed Office), GI, Critical Area
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.harfordcountymd.gov/164/Planning-Zoning).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.harfordcountymd.gov/277/GIS-Mapping.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Harford County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`aberdeen/`**: City of Aberdeen — Autonomous Municipal Development Code Chapter 235
- **`bel_air/`**: Town of Bel Air — Autonomous Zoning Code Chapter 165
- **`havre_de_grace/`**: City of Havre de Grace — Autonomous City Zoning Code Chapter 205
