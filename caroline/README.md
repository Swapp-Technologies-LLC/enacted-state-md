# Caroline County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Caroline County Commissioners
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Caroline County Code Chapter 175 (Zoning)**
- **Planning Agency:** Caroline County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** A (Agricultural), R (Rural Residential), C-1 (Neighborhood Commercial), C-2 (Highway Commercial), I (Industrial), Critical Area Overlay
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.carolinemd.org/163/Planning-Codes).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.carolinemd.org/167/GIS-Mapping.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Caroline County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`denton/`**: Town of Denton — Autonomous Municipal Zoning
- **`federalsburg/`**: Town of Federalsburg — Municipal Planning Commission
