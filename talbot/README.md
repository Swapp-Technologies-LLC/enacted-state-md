# Talbot County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Talbot County Council
- **Primary Ordinance:** **Talbot County Code Chapter 190 (Zoning, Subdivision and Land Development)**
- **Planning Agency:** Talbot County Department of Planning & Zoning.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture & Zoning Districts
- **Primary Classifications:** AC (Agricultural Conservation), CP (Conservation Permitted), WRC (Western Rural Conservation), RC, TR (Town Residential), BC (Business Commercial), Critical Area
- **Critical Area Overlay:** State-mandated 1,000-ft tidal buffer overlays enforced under Natural Resources Article § 8-1801 where applicable.

---

## 3. Primary Sourcing & Continuous Change Stream
- **Legislative Ingestion Portal:** Primary Government Source (https://www.talbotcountymd.gov/planning_and_zoning).
- **Enacted Instruments:** County Commissioners / County Council Ordinances and Resolutions.
- **GIS & Spatial Boundaries:** https://www.talbotcountymd.gov/gis.
- **Anti-Aggregator Guarantee:** Primary source government endpoints only. Commercial aggregators (Municode, AmLegal, eCode360) are blocked.

---

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Zoning Authorities
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, incorporated cities and towns in Talbot County may exercise autonomous zoning powers:
- **`easton/`**: Town of Easton — Autonomous Municipal Zoning Chapter 28
- **`st_michaels/`**: Town of St. Michaels — Independent Historic Waterfront Planning Commission
- **`oxford/`**: Town of Oxford — Independent Town Zoning
