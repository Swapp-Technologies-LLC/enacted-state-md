# Frederick County Bill No. 23-20
**Status:** Adopted/Enacted by the County Council of Frederick County  
**Adopted Date:** October 24, 2023  
**Effective Date:** December 23, 2023  

## Summary of Enacted Changes
Bill No. 23-20 amends the Frederick County Zoning Ordinance (Chapter 1-19) to permit limited landscape contractor parking and equipment storage as an accessory use in the Agricultural (A) zoning district subject to site plan approval and acreage minimums.

COUNTY COUNCIL FOR FREDERICK COUNTY, MARYLAND
Bill No. 23-20
Introduced: September 19, 2023
Adopted: October 24, 2023
AN ACT concerning: Limited Landscape Contractor Parking and Storage in the Agricultural (A) District.

SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF FREDERICK COUNTY, MARYLAND, that
Chapter 1-19 of the Frederick County Code is hereby amended to establish provisions
for limited landscape contractor parking and storage in the Agricultural (A) District,
requiring a minimum parcel size of 5 acres and a 100-foot setback from all property lines.
