# Frederick County Bill No. 23-21
**Status:** Adopted/Enacted by the County Council of Frederick County  
**Adopted Date:** November 7, 2023  
**Effective Date:** January 6, 2024  

## Summary of Enacted Changes
Bill No. 23-21 amends Chapter 1-19 of the Frederick County Code (Zoning Ordinance) to establish definitions, permitted zoning districts, and specific distance requirements for licensed cannabis dispensaries, growing facilities, and processing facilities pursuant to the Cannabis Reform Act of 2023.

COUNTY COUNCIL FOR FREDERICK COUNTY, MARYLAND
Bill No. 23-21
Introduced: October 3, 2023
Adopted: November 7, 2023
AN ACT concerning: Amendments to Chapter 1-19 of the Frederick County Code
(Zoning Ordinance) to Add Cannabis Dispensary, Cannabis Growing Facility, and
Cannabis Processing Facility.

SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF FREDERICK COUNTY, MARYLAND, that
Chapter 1-19 of the Frederick County Code is hereby amended by adding Section
1-19-8.320 and amending Section 1-19-5.310 (Use Table) to permit cannabis dispensaries
in the GC (General Commercial) and LI (Limited Industrial) districts subject to
specified buffer distances from schools and youth centers.
