# Frederick County, Maryland — Zoning Legal Repository

## 1. Regulatory Authority & Governance
- **Governing Body:** Frederick County Council.
- **Primary Ordinance:** Frederick County Code **Chapter 1-19 (Land Management Code - LMC)**.
- **Planning Agency:** Frederick County Planning and Zoning Division (`frederickcountymd.gov/planning`).
- **Charter Government:** Transitioned to Charter home rule government in December 2014.

---

## 2. Statutory Architecture (Chapter 1-19 LMC Breakdown)
The Land Management Code integrates zoning, subdivision, and environmental controls:
- **Article I: General Provisions & Administration**
- **Article II: Zoning Districts & Uses**:
  - *Agricultural & Resource:* A (Agricultural - minimum 25-acre density tracking), RC (Resource Conservation)
  - *Residential:* R-1 (Low density), R-3, R-5, R-8 (Medium density), R-12, R-16 (High density multifamily)
  - *Commercial & Industrial:* VC (Village Center), GC (General Commercial), LI (Light Industrial), GI (General Industrial)
  - *Planned Development:* PUD (Planned Unit Development), MXD (Mixed Use Development)
- **Article III: Design and Dimensional Standards** (Lot coverage, setbacks, building envelope)
- **Article IV: Environmental Standards** (Wellhead protection, slope protection >25%, forest conservation)

## 4. Municipal Subdivisions & Autonomous Jurisdictions
Under Maryland Land Use Article Division I, Frederick County has **12 incorporated municipalities**, several with independent planning and zoning powers:
- **`city_of_frederick/`**: The City of Frederick (County Seat and second-largest city in Maryland) operates with **100% autonomous zoning authority** under the **City of Frederick Land Management Code (LMC)**.
- **`brunswick/`**: Independent planning commission and municipal zoning ordinance.
- **`middletown/`**: Autonomous planning and zoning commission under Town Charter.
- **`thurmont/`, `emmitsburg/`, `walkerville/`, `myersville/`**: Incorporated towns exercising independent or concurrent local planning review.
