# City of Frederick — Autonomous Municipal Land Management Code

## 1. Regulatory Authority
- **Autonomous Zoning:** YES
- **Notes:** The City of Frederick has independent planning and zoning jurisdiction under the City LMC. Governed by the Mayor and Board of Aldermen and City Planning Commission. County Chapter 1-19 does not apply within city boundaries.
- **Sourcing:** Primary municipal portal and council agendas.
