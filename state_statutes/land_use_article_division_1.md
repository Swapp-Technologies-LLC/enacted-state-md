# Maryland Annotated Code — Land Use Article: Division I

**Title:** Single-Jurisdiction Planning and Zoning  
**Applicability:** Applies to 18 non-charter and charter counties, Baltimore City, and autonomous incorporated municipalities (Rockville, Gaithersburg, Laurel, Annapolis, Frederick, etc.).  
**Statutory Source:** Maryland General Assembly (`mgaleg.maryland.gov`).

---

## Key Provisions & Legal Authorities

### Title 1. Definitions; General Provisions
- **§ 1-201:** General rule of strict construction for local zoning powers.
- **§ 1-401:** Visions: Mandates that all local comprehensive plans incorporate 12 statewide planning visions (quality of life, public participation, growth areas, infrastructure, community design, environmental protection, resource conservation, stewardship, transportation, funding, economic development, and environmental sustainability).

### Title 3. Comprehensive Planning
- **§ 3-101:** Planning Commission creation, quorum, and powers.
- **§ 3-201:** Mandatory 10-year comprehensive master plan updates and implementation reviews.

### Title 4. Zoning Regulations & Zoning Commissions
- **§ 4-101:** Grant of zoning powers: Local legislative bodies may divide jurisdictions into zoning districts to regulate height, bulk, size of buildings, open spaces, and population density.
- **§ 4-102:** Uniformity requirement within each zoning district classification.
- **§ 4-204:** Notice and hearing requirements for zoning map and text amendments.

### Title 5. Subdivision Regulations & Plat Approval
- **§ 5-101:** Jurisdiction over land subdivisions within municipal/county boundaries.
