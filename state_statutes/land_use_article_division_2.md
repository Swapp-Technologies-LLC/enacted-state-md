# Maryland Annotated Code — Land Use Article: Division II

**Title:** Maryland-National Capital Park and Planning Commission (M-NCPPC)  
**Applicability:** Bi-county regional jurisdiction covering Montgomery County and Prince George's County (the Regional District).  
**Statutory Source:** Maryland General Assembly (`mgaleg.maryland.gov`).

---

## Key Provisions & Legal Authorities

### Title 14. M-NCPPC Organization & Administration
- **§ 14-101:** Bi-county commission structure: 10 commissioners (5 appointed from Montgomery County, 5 appointed from Prince George's County).

### Title 20. Maryland-Washington Regional District Boundaries
- **§ 20-101:** Encompasses all of Montgomery County (excluding Rockville, Gaithersburg, Poolesville, Barnesville, Laytonsville) and all of Prince George's County (excluding the City of Laurel).

### Title 21. Regional District Planning Powers & Master Plans
- **§ 21-103:** Mandatory referral requirement: All public buildings, roads, parks, and utilities must be submitted to M-NCPPC for advisory review prior to construction.

### Title 22. District Council Zoning Authority
- **§ 22-101:** Distinct powers of the County Council of Montgomery County and Prince George's County sitting as District Councils.
- **§ 22-104:** Authority to enact local Zoning Text Amendments (ZTAs) and Sectional Map Amendments (SMAs).
