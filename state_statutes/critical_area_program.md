# Maryland Annotated Code — Natural Resources Article: Title 8, Subtitle 18

**Title:** Chesapeake and Atlantic Coastal Bays Critical Area Protection Program  
**Applicability:** Statewide mandate covering all 16 coastal counties, Baltimore City, and 47 waterfront municipalities.  
**Regulatory Oversight:** Critical Area Commission for the Chesapeake and Atlantic Coastal Bays.  
**Statutory Source:** Maryland General Assembly (`mgaleg.maryland.gov`).

---

## Core Statutory Requirements

### 1. 1,000-Foot Critical Area Buffer
- Land situated within **1,000 feet of the mean high-water line** of tidal waters, tidal wetlands, and tributary streams is subject to state-supervised critical area zoning overlays.

### 2. Mandatory Land-Use Classifications
Local jurisdictions must classify all land within the Critical Area into one of three statutory management tiers:
1. **Intensely Developed Areas (IDA):** Areas where residential, commercial, or industrial development predominates. Must achieve a 10% reduction in stormwater runoff pollutants (the "10% Rule").
2. **Limited Development Areas (LDA):** Areas developed with low- to moderate-density housing, commercial activities, or open space. Maximum 15% forest clearing and 15% impervious surface cap.
3. **Resource Conservation Areas (RCA):** Nature-dominated areas (wetlands, forests, agriculture). Strict residential density cap of **1 dwelling unit per 20 acres**.

### 3. Local Implementation Mandate
Each affected Maryland jurisdiction (including Anne Arundel County Article 18 Title 5, Baltimore City Article 32 Title 8, Prince George's Subtitle 27 Part 27-8) must maintain a locally adopted Critical Area Program approved by the State Critical Area Commission.
